## What happened

The read-only research job for the electorate-level results XML came back incomplete. The
supervisor then found the 2026 feed on the host at `media.election.net.nz/xml/` (one file per
electorate, no CORS headers), captured twelve files as fixtures, and dispatched M3 increment 4 on
them. Three PRs merged on 22 October:

- [PR #62](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/62): M3 increment 4, electorate leaders,
  a hex map and conservative seat calculations on night/. Two fresh reviews. The second asked for
  screenshots the Codex sandbox cannot take; the host run of `night_browser.py` passed (44
  images), and the map at 390 px and the final seat waterfall at 1280 px (House 122, overhang 2)
  were looked at.
- [PR #61](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/61): the parties page, built from the
  accepted `pages/parties.md`. Three fresh reviews; the host screenshot harness passed on 81
  routes and 343 images. One review item (licences checked against a global set rather than per
  source) and a caption punctuation nit are deferred to the next parties round.
- [PR #63](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/63): an incident fix. The social daily
  spent its credits, then failed all 39 classification batches. Two single probes on the host
  showed why: the request asked Jev for `jev-latest`, which now answers `jev-1.14.0`, while the
  validator accepts only the reviewed `jev-1.13.0`. The request now names the pinned version from
  the same constant the validator reads, and `evals/social-model-rotation` fails on the old code
  and passes on the new. The proving run is today's 13:40Z social daily.

The 13:40Z social daily cron missed again and was dispatched by hand; the hourly succeeded at
every check. After 10:05Z the day's Codex share was spent, so the rest of the day was the
incident, a plan amendment and the next brief.

## Decisions

One judgement call, sent at 07:40Z: fetch live election-night results through a scheduled Actions
workflow that commits same-origin JSON, because the EC feed sends no CORS headers and the browser
cannot read it directly. The alternative was a proxy, which this lab has no server for. Byron was
silent, so it closed as a yes at 19:45Z and `plan-m3.md` increment 5 was amended.

## What was learned

Pin what you validate, and request what you pin. The social pipeline checked the model version
strictly but asked for a moving alias, so the check could only ever fail after the money was
spent. The eval keeps that pairing honest. The failure also cost a full day's credits before
classification ran; checking the model with one call before the collect is a cheap guard for the
next social round.

The research job missed what a direct look at the media host found in minutes. For a single
missing URL, probing the host is cheaper than a research brief.
