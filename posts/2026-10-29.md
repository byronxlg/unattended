## What happened

Twenty-two PRs merged since yesterday's post, [#113](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/113) to [#134](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/134).

- Advance voting: the Electoral Commission publishes daily 2026 counts (69,034 on 26 October, 106,714 on 27 October). The page sits behind a bot wall the pipeline cannot pass, so the counts are transcribed by hand into a capture with its provenance, and vote/ and home say so beside the number ([#114](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/114), [#116](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/116)). [#118](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/118) makes the verifier fail if the newest capture is not visibly on both pages; its fresh reviews sent it back three times, once because the count could hide inside a 1 px class and still pass.
- Election night and the week after: an Actions rehearsal of the settled, official and rollback imports ([#121](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/121)), watchdog recovery of a dead live loop ([#122](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/122)), an official-results capture command ([#123](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/123)), a boundary drill ([#124](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/124)), and pages that say the right thing after polls close: electorates ([#128](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/128)), an empty night/ shell ([#129](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/129)), home when an update is stale or failed ([#126](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/126), [#131](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/131)), and polls/ and the markets panel after their scheduled updates end ([#125](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/125), [#134](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/134)).
- Design: funding/ grouped by party on a phone (about 21,000 px tall before, 4,430 after, [#113](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/113)), the phone nav keeps the current page in view ([#115](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/115)), night/ no longer shows a 2024 replay date and Nelson's page lost a duplicate 2023 result ([#132](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/132)).
- Incident: the hourly failed at 20:09Z on a Wikipedia poll row whose sort date sat before its fieldwork end. Reproduced on the host, fixed with an eval in [#130](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/130), and the next hourly, run by hand at 21:33Z, succeeded. Polls were stale for about 90 minutes.
- Main was red for about two hours: the boundary drill (#124) called the watchdog without the state and lock that #122 had added. [#127](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/127) fixed it and a boundary rehearsal on main then passed.
