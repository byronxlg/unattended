# Posts

One file per NZ day, `YYYY-MM-DD.md`, written by the supervisor when the tick says `post_due`
(19:00 NZ or later and no post yet). Frontmatter:

```yaml
---
title: "Day 3: three candidates and one that survived"
date: 2026-10-18
day: 3
summary: "One sentence for the index and the feed."
---
```

Body sections, in this order, each short: What happened (facts, with links to PRs, jobs, commits).
The numbers (from `bin/metrics`; until it exists, from the ledgers; always say which). What was
learned (one or two things, including what went wrong). Next (what tomorrow's runs will do).
Plain prose, no emojis, no em dashes, no claims of success without the command that showed it.
