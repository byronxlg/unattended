## What happened

Six PRs merged on 23 October:

- [PR #65](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/65): M3 increment 5, live election-night
  results fetched by scheduled Actions runs and deployed as same-origin JSON, with a rehearsal mode
  and the shared publication freeze. Three fix rounds, two of them about one browser test whose
  harness raced the page's fetch on resume.
- [PR #64](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/64): the home rebuilt from the accepted
  `pages/home.md`, in question rank: dates and how to vote, the seat picture, poll movement, then
  the electorate picker. Five fix rounds and three fresh reviews. The question test at 390 px
  passed on host screenshots.
- [PR #66](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/66): parties refinement, licences checked
  per source.
- [PR #68](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/68): an incident fix. The Pages deploy of
  PR #64 failed its own verify step: home was 68,868 bytes against a 65,000 budget on the live data
  branch, while CI builds from smaller fixtures and passed. The live site kept the previous build
  until the fix. `evals/pages-home-bytes-live` fails on the old code and passes on the new (59,724
  bytes); the next deploy succeeded at 12:18Z.
- [PR #67](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/67): electorate pages, candidates first,
  the vote.nz check and the boundary locator on the first screen. Supervisor screenshots found
  candidate names in the EC sheet form ("PAUL, Tamatha-Kaye Erin") twice before they were right.
- [PR #69](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/69): the social daily spent its 7,560
  credits and then discarded the day, because its allocation is exactly 24 pages and one upstream
  retry left the last hourly window unfetchable. It now publishes what it fetched, labelled N of 24
  hours, with `evals/social-retry-budget`. CI failed once on an untouched test (a temporary
  directory cleanup in `publish.py`), passed on rerun, and the same cleanup runs in production
  publishing, so it gets its own fix.

The social daily cron missed again and was dispatched by hand; the hourly held all day.

## Decisions

No judgement calls went to Byron. Two reversible single-change calls, both in the journal: fix the
home byte budget forward rather than revert PR #64, since M2 is due on 26 October and the fix was a
trim, not a design question; and publish a partial social day rather than raise the credit cap.

## What was learned

Verify on the data readers will get. The home passed every check on fixtures and failed on the
data branch at deploy. UI briefs and host checks now build from `origin/data` before merge.

The Codex sandbox cannot start Chromium, so each browser check landed on the host a round late.
Several fix rounds came from things a browser would have shown in the first. Two lapses were
the supervisor's own: accepting fixes on a local check without reading CI, which was already red.
