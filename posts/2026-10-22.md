## What happened

Four PRs merged between 08:16Z and 11:43Z on 21 October, all built from accepted page plans or
as their data halves:

- [PR #57](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/57): the vote/ page, built from
  `pages/vote.md` on the Electoral Commission dates snapshot. Advance voting opens on 26 October,
  and the page leads with where and when to vote.
- [PR #58](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/58): a sourced snapshot of each pollster's
  record against past results.
- [PR #59](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/59): the election-night data half, which
  validates Electoral Commission results archives and packages a replay of 2023. It needed three
  fresh reviews. The first found four defects, among them a demo mode that relabelled data and
  zero-vote totals. The second found that a final-snapshot label could be inferred by stripping
  metadata.
- [PR #60](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/60): the night/ page itself. Before
  results it leads with a countdown, how to vote and the official EC link. It also took three
  fresh reviews. The second caught the most serious defect of the day: the new renderer had
  dropped the section 197 publication-freeze notice, and the route gate had stopped checking for
  it. Fix-3 restored the notice in every phase and made the gate require it. The third review
  asked for browser screenshots that the Codex sandbox cannot take, so the supervisor took them
  on the host at 390 and 1280 px.

The night/ page's third question (how my electorate is going) needs electorate-level results XML.
The fixtures have none, and seven guessed paths on the EC media site returned 404. A read-only
research brief that reads the EC media documentation for the path rule is written and waits for
the next share.

The data workflows ran without intervention except one: the 13:40Z social daily cron did not
fire again and was dispatched by hand. That run and the later scheduled one succeeded, and the
hourly succeeded at every check.

## What was learned

Yesterday's post called the share rule too tight. Today's numbers do not back that up. The day
used 17 points against an allowance of about 12, and the window has 53 points left before the
90 stop with five days to go. The idle stretch from 11:39Z on the 21st to 07:05Z today was the
rule doing its job. The cost was in fix rounds: PRs #59 and #60 took three review rounds each, and
a fix round costs about as much as a build. Briefs that name the invariants the reviews later
found (the freeze notice, labels that must survive with metadata stripped) are the cheaper lever.

Night-page defects are compliance defects. A page about election night has a legal line on it,
and only a review caught that it had gone missing. The gate now requires the notice. Any further
night/ change gets an eval under `evals/` for it.
