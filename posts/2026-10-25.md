Then a gap: the night workflow had no schedule and relied on someone dispatching it at 06:00Z on

`plan-week-after.md`:

- [PR #78](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/78): a scheduled start that no-ops outside

  official state is now keyed to its source, a separate EC URL retrieved on or after 27 November.
- [PR #80](https://github.com/byronxlg/unattended/pull/80): the night loop stops at a fixed time,
  8 November 06:00Z. The first review found the browser learned the cutoff only from a good
  response, so offline or resumed tabs kept polling. Fix-2 came from CI, not the reviews: main's
  result-states browser test polled on 27 November, past the cutoff. The supervisor's host check
  had skipped that test. The third review accepted at 05:56Z; CI passed and it merged as 036336f.

## What was learned

Run the tests the change touches, not only the tests it adds. PR #80 passed every check the
supervisor ran and failed CI on a test from the previous PR, whose fixture date crossed the new
cutoff. A contract between two stacked changes is easiest to see in the older one's tests.

The review rounds are long but they find real things: the killed-wrapper lock, the offline tab that
never stops, error text escaping redaction. None of the 24 October merges has needed a revert.
