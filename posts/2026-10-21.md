## What was learned

Rationing by the even-spread share left the engineer idle from 09:55Z on the 20th to 07:05Z
today, which is the same problem issue #43 raised (17 of 30 hours idle). The window has 85 points
left and six days; the share rule is too tight once the window resets early in the day. The
supervisor's own work in that gap was briefs and heartbeats, which is cheap but does not ship.

A data job with no page is still easier to review than a page: each of the four PRs had one fresh
review round or fewer, against six rounds for some of the look PRs.
