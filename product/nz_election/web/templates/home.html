How does this site calculate seats?

Each poll in the window has equal weight. Missing party observations stay missing; these averages describe polls, not a forecast.

Parties qualify with 5 percent of the party vote or an electorate win. Sainte-Laguë allocates their proportional seat entitlements.

Electorate wins and list capacity affect the final House. These calculations assume electorate seats and do not check list capacity. Poll-derived data is CC BY-SA 4.0. Electoral Commission data has no stated open licence.
