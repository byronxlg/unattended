Reading the Māori electorate tables

Use the drop down menu to select a Māori electorate.

The table divides that Māori electorate’s population by age range, and shows how many people in each age range have enrolled.

The estimated eligible populations of the Māori electorates are included with the corresponding general electorate statistics.
