## Readers and their questions

Task-focused search arrivals and home referrals: enrolment to 25 October, overseas from 21 October, in-person from 26 October to 7 November. The page answers in one screen and links out to the Electoral Commission for anything that changes.

1. Am I enrolled, and is it too late? - to 25 Oct - evidence: https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/about/overview/key-dates, https://blog.google/intl/en-nz/products/2023_12_year-in-search-heres-what-kiwis/
2. Where can I vote today, and when is it open? - 26 Oct to 7 Nov - evidence: https://elections.nz/assets/2023-General-Election/voter-and-non-voter-survey-2023.pdf
3. What do I bring, and how do my two votes work? - first-time and lapsed voters - evidence: https://elections.nz/assets/2023-General-Election/voter-and-non-voter-survey-2023.pdf
4. How do I vote overseas or get help if I cannot attend? - smaller, high consequence - evidence: https://vote.nz/2026-general-election/overseas/vote-from-overseas, https://elections.nz/assets/2023-General-Election/voter-and-non-voter-survey-2023.pdf
5. Where can I compare candidates and policies? - undecided - evidence: https://elections.nz/assets/2023-General-Election/voter-and-non-voter-survey-2023.pdf, https://policy.nz/2026

## Answers

1. Deadline sentence plus "Check or update your enrolment" link to vote.nz.
2. "Use the official finder for places and opening hours" link; the number of days until election day.
3. Three short lines: no EasyVote card needed; party vote and electorate vote; link to official guidance.
4. Links: overseas, accessible formats, assisted voting, each with its NZ-time deadline.
5. Link to the reader's electorate page and to Policy.nz.

## Data, worked back

1 to 4. Fetch once: EC pages (key dates, what to do, overseas, accessible formats), EC copyright, independent summary and links only, reviewed daily to 7 Nov.
5. Have: electorate routes; link only to Policy.nz.

## Page order

At 390, above the fold: answer 1 (to 25 Oct) or answer 2 (from 26 Oct) first, the other second, then 3. Below: 4, then 5. No chart.

## Not shown

An eligibility checker, stored addresses, copied voting-place hours (official services own them), any voting recommendation. Neutral voting information stays live on election day under s197; no additions after midnight 6 Nov.

## Question test

From 390x844 alone a reviewer answers 1 to 3 in five seconds each, and every link in the first screen goes to an official page.
