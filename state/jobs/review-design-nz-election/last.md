1. **Important: the first viewport does not fit the existing strip geometry.** [design.md:93](intent/nz-election/design.md:93) promises everything above the fold, but 390 px leaves **358 px** after gutters. Budget roughly **82 px masthead**, **141-176 px headline** (four or five lines at 32/35.2, not two), **104 px strip**, **408 px label lane**, **21 px majority label**, **63-83 px provenance**, plus approximately **100 px spacing**: **919-974 px**, exceeding 844 px. The lane height is computed using the existing packing function for NAT 44, LAB 34, GRN 13, TPM 12, ACT 9, NZF 8: four overlapping label rows, `4 × 96 + 24 = 408` ([build.py:114](product/nz_election/pipeline/build.py:114), [styles.css:5](product/nz_election/web/styles.css:5)). Provenance falls below the fold; the majority label may too. Existing navigation, state bands and the table returned with the strip push it further down. Specify a compact external-label layout, shorter headline, collapsed phone navigation and provenance before the detailed table. Test at **360 px** too, required by [REVIEW.md:14](REVIEW.md:14).

3. **Important: the headline invents coalitions, and “61 to govern” overclaims the calculation.** [design.md:101](intent/nz-election/design.md:101) conflicts with the prohibition on predicted coalition defaults in [spec.md:41](intent/nz-election/spec.md:41). The explorer’s default is explicitly `selected=[]` ([coalitions.py:90](product/nz_election/pipeline/coalitions.py:90)). Use party totals under its default electorate assumptions, with these exact forms: **majority:** “{Party}: {N} calculated seats, reaching the assumed majority of {M}.” **No majority:** “{Party} leads with {N} calculated seats; no party reaches the assumed majority of {M}.” **No data:** “No seat estimate available yet. Read how seats are calculated.” For tied leaders: “{Party A} and {Party B} are level on {N} calculated seats; no party reaches the assumed majority of {M}.” Put polling dates and the assumptions link directly beneath. Derive **M** from `assumed_majority`, including overhang; label the marker “{M} for a majority”, not a guarantee of government formation.

4. **Important: the provenance chip is both too repetitive and insufficient as the sole source note.** “Every number” at [design.md:136](intent/nz-election/design.md:136) would create 71 repeated chips, potentially several per row, while omitting original-source, licence and calculation-input links required by [spec.md:244](intent/nz-election/spec.md:244). **Tables:** one shared note beside the caption, with row references only where sources or dates differ. **Strips:** one immediately below the graphic, before the equivalent table. **Single numbers:** one adjacent note. **Maps:** boundary attribution under the map, plus a separate source/date for the selected result. Link each group programmatically to its note. Keep fieldwork/result dates distinct from retrieval dates; make downloads an additional destination, not the only source link.

5. **Important: stale-state timing and copy misrepresent pipeline status.** [design.md:150](intent/nz-election/design.md:150) makes daily data overdue after **72 hours**, whereas [spec.md:257](intent/nz-election/spec.md:257) requires **36 hours**; hourly is three hours and live is 90 seconds. Age alone does not establish “the last one failed”. [publish.py:106](product/nz_election/pipeline/publish.py:106) preserves attempt chronology independently of successful acquisition, and distinguishes failed refresh with retained data from **unavailable with no previous success**. Define these separately. Use “Update overdue. Last checked {time}” unless an actual failed attempt is recorded. “This page then shows polls to that hour” also confuses acquisition with fieldwork dates.

6. **Important: the state inventory misses existing production paths.** [design.md:144](intent/nz-election/design.md:144) needs explicit treatments for **offline verification**, **polls present but seats unavailable**, **stale seats withheld**, **incomplete explorer inputs**, **invalid or outdated shared scenarios**, **invalid electorate counts retaining the last valid result**, **unresolved allocation ties**, and **candidate-source conflicts / unresolved boundary comparisons**. These already appear in [build.py:174](product/nz_election/pipeline/build.py:174), [coalitions.mjs:154](product/nz_election/web/coalitions.mjs:154) and [electorate_pages.py:43](product/nz_election/pipeline/electorate_pages.py:43). “The numbers stay” must not resurrect a stale allocation as current. Translate reader-facing “family”, “bundle”, “refresh workflow” and “candidate baseline” into “polls”, “previous data”, “update details” and “2023 candidate results”; reserve technical identifiers for source details.

7. **Important: the strip instructions contradict the retained accessibility rule.** [design.md:65](intent/nz-election/design.md:65) prohibits text on party fills, but [design.md:119](intent/nz-election/design.md:119) asks for counts inside fitting segments, reinforced by the wireframe. Specify one treatment: external ink-on-paper labels connected to segments, with its actual height budget. Existing white label patches inside segments are a different treatment and need explicit resolution against [spec.md:274](intent/nz-election/spec.md:274). Also correct the obsolete “ACT yellow” and “NZ First black” descriptions: the sampled primary fills are pink and navy.

8. **Important: “leading party” on the map has no defined comparable dataset.** [design.md:128](intent/nz-election/design.md:128) does not say candidate vote versus party vote, election year, or boundary basis. Existing detail pages explicitly preserve **2023 original boundaries**, without reallocating votes to 2025 electorates ([electorate.html:7](product/nz_election/web/templates/electorate.html:7)). Use a neutral selected outline for M2. Introduce party fills only with a named measure, dated source and matching geography; otherwise the map implies a current electorate leader the data cannot establish.

9. **Important: the night waterfall implies a known remaining vote total.** “Remaining share of votes” and “percent counted” at [design.md:130](intent/nz-election/design.md:130) risk converting reporting-place coverage into vote completion. [spec.md:53](intent/nz-election/spec.md:53) explicitly forbids that. Say **“{X}% of voting places reported”** when that is the measured quantity; do not draw a remaining-votes box unless the source supplies a defensible denominator. Replay, illustrative replay and unavailable-live states also need visible distinctions before the orange “live” indicator is specified.

10. **Nit: keep system sans for this iteration; the font is affordable in principle, but not justified yet.** No `site/nz-election` build exists. Measured templates: **base 638 B, polls 58 B; CSS 8,909 B**. With a 45,000 B font, that is **54,605 B before template expansion**. CSS gzips to **2,526 B**; shell templates are not rendered-page transfer measurements. The font alone consumes 45% of the decimal 100 KB budget. Existing explorer modules add **12,177 B + 6,203 B raw**, plus startup JSON fetches, omitted from [design.md:181](intent/nz-election/design.md:181). Self-hosting satisfies **no font CDN**; the brief explicitly supersedes the old Design section’s no-font-download rule. Thus there is no demonstrated budget failure, but no verified pass either. Four display uses do not yet demonstrate enough value: spend the remaining budget on readable data and fast loading. Reconsider after a real-content comparison proves a benefit, including fallback wrapping and font-loading behaviour.

11. **Nit: some styling rules substitute a familiar aesthetic for a subject-specific decision.** Universal square corners and ledger hairlines ([design.md:23](intent/nz-election/design.md:23)) can still produce the generic broadsheet the frontend-design skill warns about. Orange ticks on every link also borrow ballot-selection meaning for ordinary navigation, and conflict with the night section reserving orange for “live”. Keep the distinctive proportional seat composition; use ticks for actual selection and ordinary underlines for links. Byron’s staged track ([intent.md:96](intent/nz-election/intent.md:96)) leaves room to validate the night treatment later.
